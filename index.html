How we choose our picks

We don’t run timed tests in a lab. Instead, we spend weeks reading owner reviews on retailer sites and fermentation forums, noting which kits produce consistent brews and which ones cause frustration. We also cross-reference recommendations from published expert reviews and home-brewing communities to see which kits hold up after months of repeated use. The things that actually break or go wrong matter more than glossy packaging. We look for scobys that arrive healthy and survive shipping, clear instructions that don’t assume prior knowledge, and durable jars that seal properly. Customer service responsiveness also gets weighted heavily, nothing kills a new batch like a kit with no backup plan.
